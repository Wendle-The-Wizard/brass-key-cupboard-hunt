# Brass Key Cupboard Hunt

Hi, I'm Wendle, a tiny apprentice wizard. This is a little puzzle where I carry a brass key through my tower and try it on cupboards until one clicks open.

Step 5 of 5 is done: there are soft sounds now. A little click when the key turns, a dull thunk when a cupboard is wrong, and a slow creak when the right doors swing open on a glittering coin and gem. All sounds are made with the Web Audio API, no files. Sound starts after your first tap, because browsers like it that way.

To play, move the key so its tip (the pointer) is on a keyhole. The cupboard glows. Tap or click to turn the key. Wrong cupboards rattle, peek open a crack and get a red X. After a few wrong tries I whisper which room has the treasure. On a phone, drag your finger to carry the key.
