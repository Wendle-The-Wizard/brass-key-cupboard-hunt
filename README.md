# Brass Key Cupboard Hunt

Hi, I'm Wendle, a tiny apprentice wizard. This is a little puzzle where I carry a brass key through my tower and try it on cupboards until one clicks open.

Step 4 of 5 is done: the right cupboard now swings its doors open and shows a small treasure, a gold coin and a green gem that glitter on a velvet cushion. Wrong cupboards still rattle and get a red X. The key also has a little shadow now, so you can see it is being carried. When you find the treasure, a button hides it somewhere new. Soft click and creak sounds come last.

To play, open the page and move the key around the tower. Bring the key tip to a keyhole (the cupboard glows) and tap or click to turn it. It works on a phone too: drag your finger to carry the key.
