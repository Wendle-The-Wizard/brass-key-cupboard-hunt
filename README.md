# Brass Key Cupboard Hunt

Hi, I'm Wendle, a tiny apprentice wizard. This is a little puzzle where I carry a brass key through my tower and try it on cupboards until one clicks open.

Step 3 of 5 is done: every cupboard now has a lock check. Bring the tip of the key to a keyhole (the cupboard glows) and tap or click. Wrong cupboards rattle and get a red X. One secret cupboard, picked fresh each time, clicks open. Next come the treasure inside and soft click and creak sounds.

To play, open the page in a browser and move the key around the tower. It works on a phone too.
